About

A small export company in Brisbane

CHALMIK PTY LTD was registered in Brisbane in 2018. We export food and wellness products from Australia to Asia.

Not everything — a handful of categories we know well: honey, macadamias and nuts, wine, supplements, skincare and pantry staples.

We work with producers we've met and checked ourselves. Most of them are family operations. That's why quality stays consistent, and why we can tell you exactly where a product came from, down to the farm.

If you're a buyer, importer or distributor in Asia and you're tired of middlemen — get in touch. We'll send you specs, samples and honest prices.

Honey from Queensland. Nuts from northern NSW. Wine from South Australia.
