About us

Brisbane, Australia

We export food and wellness products from Australia to Asia.

We keep the range deliberately small — only the categories we know well, from producers we've met and checked ourselves. Most of them are family operations. That's why quality stays consistent, and why we can tell you exactly where a product came from, down to the farm.

If you're a buyer, importer or distributor in Asia and you're tired of middlemen — get in touch. We'll send you specs, samples and honest prices.

What we export

Six categories. Done properly.

Honey & bee products 01

Raw, creamed and manuka honey from Queensland and WA apiaries. Retail jars or bulk drums.

Macadamias & nuts 02

Australian macadamias and almonds, raw or roasted. Mostly from family growers in Queensland and northern NSW.

Wine & beverages 03

Table wines from established South Australian and Victorian wineries. Single bottles to full pallets.

Health & wellness 04

Vitamins and supplements made in Australia to TGA standards. Export-ready labelling.

Skincare 05

Australian-made skincare using native botanicals. We work with a few small manufacturers we know.

Oats, grains & pantry 06

Australian oats, grains and pantry staples — bulk or retail-ready packs.
